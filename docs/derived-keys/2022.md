## The second solver changed 15 answers

The first draft, cross-checked against Gemini 1.5 Pro, was wrong on fifteen
questions. All fifteen are corrected here and itemised in
[`2022-crosscheck.md`](2022-crosscheck.md). Most were **wrong option letters with
correct reasoning** — the specific error class that only a second pass catches.

Two outstanding disputes are defects in the paper, not in the solving:

- **Q40** — the correct value is y(−3) ≈ 4.97; no option matches (nearest is 4).
- **Q59** — no reading of the lens-plus-mirror combination gives F = 25 cm.

## Where it is still weak

- **Nineteen entries remain provisional.** Several are cases where the second
  solver's reasoning agreed with ours but its answer label did not, or where we
  overruled a clear misreading of a figure. Those are the ones a human should
  sample-check first.
- **Six figure questions** (Q44, Q49, Q55, Q57, Q62, Q69) were answered from page
  images. They are scoreable on the strength of the answer text but still need a
  redrawn figure beside them (ADR-0002) before the site can show them properly.
- **Q48 and Q59 have printing defects** — Q48's options omit the square root, and
  Q59's option set contains no consistent value. (D) is recorded for Q48 as the
  intended answer with the defect noted.
