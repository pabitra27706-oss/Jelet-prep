## 📖 Detailed Syllabus

### 1. MATHEMATICS

**A. Matrices (up to order 3)**  
Types, equality, addition, subtraction, multiplication, transpose, symmetric/skew‑symmetric, singular/non‑singular, adjoint, inverse, eigenvalues, Cayley‑Hamilton theorem (statement & application), diagonalisation.

**B. Determinants (up to order 3)**  
Expansion, minors, cofactors, properties, Cramer’s rule (3 unknowns), rank of matrix, homogeneous & non‑homogeneous systems (statements & applications).

**C. Complex Numbers**  
Cartesian, polar & exponential forms, modulus, amplitude, conjugate, algebra, cube roots of unity, De Moivre’s theorem (statement & simple problems).

**D. Co‑ordinate Geometry (2D)**  
Polar coordinates, relation to Cartesian, conic sections – definition, properties, tangents & normals.

**E. Vector Algebra**  
Position vector, ratio formula, resolution, dot & cross products (properties & applications: work, projection, area, moment), scalar & vector triple products, linear combination, dependence/independence.

**F. Differential Calculus**  
Function, domain/range, limit, continuity, standard limits, types of discontinuity, derivatives (1st & 2nd order), Rolle’s theorem, MVT, indeterminate forms, L’Hôpital’s rule.

**G. Application of Derivative**  
Geometric meaning, rate measurement, maxima/minima (one variable).

**H. Partial Differentiation**  
Definition, evaluation, homogeneous functions, Euler’s theorem (2 variables, without proof).

**I. Integral Calculus**  
Integration as inverse of differentiation, standard integrals, substitution, by parts, partial fractions, definite integral & properties, area under a curve.

**J. Ordinary Differential Equations**  
Order, degree. First order first degree: separation of variables, homogeneous, exact, linear. First order but not first degree. Linear second order with constant coefficients – CF, PI for polynomial, eᵃˣ, sin ax, cos ax, eᵃˣV.

**K. Probability**  
Random experiment, sample space, events, classical definition, total probability, compound probability, Bayes’ theorem (statements & simple problems).

### 2. PHYSICS

- **Units & dimensions:** CGS, MKS, SI, dimensional analysis, errors, significant figures.
- **Kinematics:** Speed, velocity, acceleration, rectilinear/circular motion, vectors.
- **Laws of motion:** Newton’s laws, force, momentum, inertia, moment of inertia, impulse, couples, equilibrium, conservation of momentum, centripetal/centrifugal forces, angular motion, torque, friction, banking of roads.
- **Work, power, energy:** Definitions, conservation, kinetic & potential energy.
- **Gravitation:** Universal law, variation of g, gravitational potential energy, projectile, vertical circular motion.
- **Elasticity:** Stress‑strain, Hooke’s law, Young’s modulus, bulk modulus, rigidity modulus, Poisson’s ratio, elastic energy.
- **Surface tension:** Cohesive/adhesive forces, surface energy, angle of contact, droplets, bubbles, capillarity, effect of impurity/temperature.
- **Fluid mechanics:** Pascal’s law, hydraulic lift, buoyancy, Archimedes’ principle, streamline/turbulent flow, continuity equation, Bernoulli’s theorem, viscosity, Stokes’ law, terminal velocity.
- **Thermal expansion:** Linear, areal, volume expansion, coefficient relations, density change with temperature.
- **Heat transfer:** Conduction, convection, radiation, thermal conductivity.
- **Thermodynamics:** Thermal equilibrium, calorimetry, zeroth law, heat, work, internal energy, first law, specific heats of gas, isothermal/isobaric/isochoric/adiabatic processes.
- **Reflection of light:** Plane mirror, image formation.
- **Refraction of light:** Laws, refractive index, total internal reflection, critical angle, optical fibre.
- **Lens:** Convex & concave lenses, image formation, lens formula, power of lens, equivalent focal length & power of two thin lenses in contact.
- **Photoelectricity:** Photoemission, work function, photoelectric current vs intensity/frequency, stopping potential, threshold frequency, photon, Einstein’s equation, solar photovoltaic cell.

---

### 3. CHEMISTRY

**A. Atomic Structure**  
Bohr model, de Broglie wave, quantum numbers, Aufbau, Pauli’s exclusion, Hund’s rule, electronic configuration, isotopes, isotones, isobars, hybridisation (sp³, sp², sp) & molecular shapes.

**B. Chemical Bonding**  
Electrovalent, covalent, coordinate bonds, hydrogen bonding, crystalline & amorphous solids, covalent bonds in C, Si, Ge.

**C. Acids, Bases & Salts**  
Mole concept, Arrhenius & Lewis concepts, neutralisation, hydrolysis, equivalent weight, concentration units (normality, molarity, molality, formality), pH scale, indicators, buffer solution, solubility product, common ion effect.

**D. Oxidation, Reduction, Electrochemistry**  
Redox balancing (ion‑electron method), redox titration, electrolysis, Faraday’s laws, electrolysis of CuSO₄, electroplating, electrorefining, electrochemical cells (dry cell, lead storage cell), electrochemical series.

**E. Chemical Equilibrium**  
Reversible/irreversible reactions, Le Chatelier’s principle, industrial processes: Haber’s process, Ostwald’s process, Contact process, catalysis.

**F. Metallurgy**  
Minerals, ores, gangue, flux, slag, extraction of Fe, Cu, Al. Alloys – composition & uses (brass, bronze, stainless steel, etc.), amalgams, cast iron, wrought iron, steel.

**G. Water**  
Hardness (types, causes, units, disadvantages), EDTA estimation, removal by Permutit, ion‑exchange, phosphate conditioning, Calgon treatment.

**H. Organic Chemistry**  
Classification, homologous series, functional groups, isomerism, IUPAC nomenclature. Preparation & properties of methane, ethylene, acetylene, methylated spirit, rectified spirit, power alcohol, proof spirit. Uses of benzene, naphthalene, phenol.

---

### 4. FUNDAMENTALS OF ELECTRICAL & ELECTRONICS ENGINEERING

- **Components & instruments:** Resistors, capacitors, inductors, PMMC & MI instruments, Wheatstone bridge.
- **Transformers:** Principle, construction, BH characteristic, transformation ratio, equivalent circuit, losses, regulation, efficiency, auto‑transformer.
- **Electrical machines:** Types of motors, rotating magnetic field, DC motor & single‑phase induction motor (construction, working, torque‑speed characteristics, speed control).
- **DC circuits:** Voltage/current sources, source transformation, Thevenin’s & Norton’s theorems, Kirchhoff’s laws, superposition, time‑domain behaviour of reactive components.
- **AC circuits:** Amplitude, frequency, phase, peak/average/RMS values, phasor representation, impedance, power factor, active/reactive power, star‑delta connection, simple RLC circuits.
- **Semiconductor devices:** Energy bands, intrinsic/extrinsic, P‑type, N‑type, PN junction diode, PNP & NPN transistors, MOSFET, CMOS.
- **Analog circuits:** Op‑amp, virtual ground, inverting/non‑inverting amplifiers, adder, subtractor, integrator, differentiator.
- **Digital electronics:** Boolean algebra, basic gates (AND, OR, NOT, NAND, NOR, EX‑OR, EX‑NOR) – truth tables, symbols, combinations.
